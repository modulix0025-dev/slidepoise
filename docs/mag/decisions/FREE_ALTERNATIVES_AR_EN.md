# بدائل مجانية لكل حاجة ليها ترخيص تجاري · Free replacements for every commercially-licensed component

> قرار المشغّل (2026-10-11): «موافق على كله إلا أي حاجة ليها ترخيص تجاري — شوف البديل المجاني الأفضل وضيفه بدالها».
> Operator decision: approve the whole plan, except that anything carrying a commercial licence is replaced by the best free alternative.
> Rule applied from now on: **no component that is dual-licensed with a paid commercial licence, sold, fee-gated for commercial users, or "non-commercial" may ship in the Studio or be its default.** Each replacement below was checked against a primary source (licence file in the official repository at the listed commit) unless marked otherwise.

## ملحوظة مهمة خارج نطاق الاستوديو · Important note outside the Studio's scope

The operator's ComfyUI workflows on the Console (the per-order pipeline) may be using **FLUX.1 Kontext [dev]** (there is a Flux Kontext dev fp8 prompt skill in this account). If they are, paid orders count as revenue-generating use under that licence, which requires a commercial licence from Black Forest Labs. The Studio **does not touch the per-order pipeline**. Phase 8a adds a **read-only** check to the Kit's probe: it lists the checkpoints each Console workflow references and their licences, and reports any non-commercial ones together with the free alternative (Qwen-Image-Edit-2511). Switching is your decision.
